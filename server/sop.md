# New Hire Onboarding — Standard Operating Procedures (FAQ)

This document is the sole source of truth for answering candidate questions about onboarding
document collection. Only answer questions using the information below. If a question falls
outside this scope, it is NOT in scope and should be escalated to HR.

## 1. Submission Deadline

All required onboarding documents must be submitted within **10 business days** of receiving
the initial document request email. Extensions can be granted only by HR directly — the agent
cannot grant extensions.

## 2. Accepted File Formats

We accept PDF, JPG, and PNG files for all document uploads. Please make sure scans or photos
are clear, well-lit, and show all four corners of the document. Files must be under 10MB each.
Multiple documents can be attached to a single email.

## 3. Who to Contact for Help

For technical issues (e.g., trouble attaching files, email bouncing), contact IT Support at
it-support@example.com. For questions about pay, benefits, or start date, contact your HR
coordinator directly — do not attempt to answer these; escalate them.

## 4. What Each Document Is

- **Passport**: Used for identity and, if applicable, work authorization verification (Form I-9).
  A valid, unexpired passport is required if provided as your primary ID document.
- **Driver's License**: Accepted as a secondary form of photo ID for I-9 verification, paired
  with a Social Security card or similar.
- **Transcript**: An official or unofficial copy of your academic transcript from the
  institution where you earned your most recent degree, used to confirm your education history.
- **EAD (Employment Authorization Document)**: Required only for candidates who are not U.S.
  citizens or permanent residents and need proof of work authorization.
- **Degree Certificate**: A copy of your diploma or degree certificate confirming completion of
  your degree program.

## 5. Do I Need to Submit All Documents at Once?

No — you can send documents across multiple emails as you gather them. The system tracks what
has been received and will let you know if anything is still outstanding.

## 6. What Happens After I Submit Everything?

Once all required documents are received, your onboarding document collection is marked
complete and no further reminders will be sent. HR will follow up separately with next steps
(equipment provisioning, first-day logistics, etc.) — those details are outside the scope of
this document collection process.
